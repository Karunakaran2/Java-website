About Us

Centre for Public Administration Reinvention and Transformation (karan)

The Centre for Public Administration Reinvention and Transformation (karan) is dedicated to redefining and reshaping governance in India. With a vision to strengthen public systems through knowledge, innovation, and reform, karan serves as a hub for research-driven consulting, institutional restructuring, and capacity enhancement in the public sector.

Bringing together experts in public policy, economics, and governance, karan designs practical, data-informed, and sustainable solutions that help government departments adapt to emerging socio-economic and digital realities. Through research, reform, and collaboration, karan empowers institutions to become more transparent, responsive, and citizen-centric.

karan, the Centre for Public Administration Reinvention and Transformation, is a premier governance think tank and consulting institution based in Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala. It serves as a facilitator and knowledge hub, bringing together policymakers, administrators, academicians and domain experts to explore, deliberate and advance the frontiers of public administration. Through its focus on research, reform and strategic transformation, karan drives evidence-based governance innovation and institutional excellence.
