About Us

Centre for Public Administration Reinvention
and Transformation (karan)

Based in Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala, karan is dedicated to strengthening governance and the public sector. With deep expertise and strong institutional partnerships, we deliver practical, citizen-centric solutions through consulting, capacity building, research, and innovation—bridging strategy, technology, and policy to drive efficiency, transparency, and impactful transformation.

Get to Know Us

Our Vision

To lead the transformation of public administration by fostering institutional collaboration, excelling in the domain, and conducting world-class research—ensuring effective governance that addresses real challenges and promotes overall excellence.

Our Mission

To empower government and public sector enterprises through strategic insights and collaborative action, driving transformative improvements in governance and service delivery while ensuring inclusiveness, effectiveness, transparency, and accountability.

Domain Expertise Driving Governance

KRAN has setup three exclusive
Centres of Excellence

karan, the sister concern of karan, extends the organization’s legacy of excellence into governance transformation. With deep domain expertise and strategic consulting, karan complements karan in driving innovation and efficiency across public sector operations.

Through research-driven insights and policy-oriented interventions, karan focuses on strengthening key governance domains — General Administration, Public Finance, and Licensing & Compliance. It reimagines administrative processes, enhances institutional frameworks, and promotes data-informed decision-making to build responsive and transparent governance systems.

Public Finance

Single source of truth for the financials of a State.

Public Finance

Public Finance is a vital pillar of effective governance and sustainable service delivery. At karan, we apply domain expertise to strengthen all aspects of public finance — from revenue mobilization and expenditure management to debt sustainability and fiscal accountability. Through research, reform, and capacity-building, we help governments create transparent and resilient financial systems that deliver measurable impact.

Licensing & Compliance

Better and faster service delivery to the citizens of the State.

Licensing & Compliance

Licensing and Compliance Management ensures regulatory efficiency and institutional accountability. At karan, we streamline permits, licensing, and compliance through process re-engineering, policy reform, and capacity enhancement. Our approach enables transparent, citizen-centric, and technology-driven regulatory frameworks that bring greater order and efficiency to governance.

General Administration

Improved efficiency within the organization and departments.

General Administration

General Administration drives efficiency and coordination across organizations and government entities. At karan, we provide expertise in planning, organizing, and managing administrative operations in areas like human resources and financial governance. Through structural reforms, process optimization, and leadership development, we help institutions enhance administrative effectiveness and governance outcomes.
